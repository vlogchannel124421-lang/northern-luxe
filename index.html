Questions

Frequently asked

Do you offer mobile car detailing?

Yes. We serve Dufferin, Shelburne, Orangeville, Caledon and Brampton. Message us on Instagram to book a visit to your home or workplace.

What is the difference between a wash and a full detail?

A wash cleans the surface. A full detail also covers the interior, engine bay, glass, wheels and a protective finish on the paint.

What is ceramic coating and is it worth it?

A protective layer on the paint that adds depth and gloss and makes the surface easier to keep clean. The right option depends on your vehicle, so we recommend a quick consultation.

Can you remove swirl marks and light scratches?

Yes. Paint correction removes swirls and light marks. We inspect the paint first and explain what can be improved.
